Privacy policy

How enquiry information is collected, used, stored and protected — in plain language.

Last updated 25 November 2026

Who is responsible

This site is operated by Atikul Islam Rabbi, Founder & CEO of Cyber Infinity, based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. For any privacy question, use the contact form.

What is collected

  • Enquiry details you submit — name, email address, selected service, urgency, asset name (optional) and the message you write.
  • Technical data — standard analytics such as pages viewed, approximate region, device type and referral source, if analytics is enabled and you consent where required.
  • Communication records — the email or WhatsApp correspondence that follows an enquiry.

Passwords, one-time codes and recovery codes are never requested and should never be sent.

Why it is processed

  • To answer your enquiry and assess whether the service applies (legitimate interest, and steps taken at your request).
  • To deliver agreed work and keep records of it (contract).
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations (legal obligation).
  • To measure site performance and improve it (consent, where required).

These providers may process data outside your country. Where required, transfers rely on the providers' standard contractual safeguards.

How long it is kept

Enquiries that do not become engagements are kept for up to 12 months, then deleted. Engagement records are kept for as long as the relationship continues and for the period required by law afterwards. Analytics data is retained according to the analytics provider's settings.

Cookies and analytics

The site itself sets no cookies. Google Tag Manager and any analytics tags it loads may set cookies or read identifiers. Where consent is required, tags should only run after consent; you can block them at any time using your browser settings or a content blocker.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may request access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to processing. Use the contact form and state what you need. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

This site is served over HTTPS, publishes no databases or credentials, and keeps the intake surface deliberately small. Access to enquiry correspondence is limited to the operator. No system is perfectly secure, and no method of transmission is guaranteed, but the practical exposure is kept to what is necessary to answer you.

Client confidentiality

Case details are published only with permission. Where a client cannot be named, identifying details are generalised. Screenshots and documents are redacted before they are stored or shared.

Children

The services are intended for businesses and adults. The site is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date above always reflects the current version.
