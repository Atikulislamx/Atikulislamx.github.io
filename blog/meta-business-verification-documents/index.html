Meta Business Verification: What Passes

The documents, name matching and domain checks that decide a Meta business verification — and the recurring reasons applications are rejected.

By Atikul Islam Rabbi Last updated 20 October 2026

Business verification is not a formality and it is not discretionary. It is a document-matching exercise. When the legal name, address, phone and domain all point to the same entity, approval is routine. When they do not, applications fail repeatedly with no useful explanation.

The four things Meta is checking

  1. Legal existence. The business is registered somewhere, and the document shows it.
  2. Name match. The name on the document matches the name in Business Manager.
  3. Address match. A document shows the address entered in the business profile.
  4. Control. You can act for the business — typically proven by domain or phone control.

Documents that are usually accepted

PurposeExamplesNotes
Legal entityCertificate of incorporation, trade licence, tax registrationMust show the registered name and, ideally, an address
AddressUtility bill, bank statement, leaseRecent (usually within 90 days), full document, not cropped
PhoneNumber able to receive SMS or a callUse a business line, not a personal mobile that changes
DomainDNS TXT record, meta tag, or a file on the siteDomain should match the business, not a free subdomain

The seven reasons applications fail

  • Trading name vs legal name. "Arpa Foods" on the site, "Arpa Food Products Ltd" on the certificate. Use the legal name in Business Manager.
  • Translated documents. A certified translation is often required; a photo of a document in another script may be refused.
  • Old address proofs. Statements older than the accepted window are treated as missing.
  • Cropped or watermarked documents. Submit the whole page, unedited.
  • Shared infrastructure. A free website builder subdomain or a generic email domain weakens the control signal.
  • Mismatched phone. The number entered does not match the business record.
  • Retrying without changes. Resubmitting identical documents produces identical results.
Do this first: write the legal name, registered address, business phone and domain on one page. Every field in Business Manager must match that page exactly before you submit.

Before and after verification

Verification unlocks ad spend limits, certain features and a stronger footing in disputes — but it does not fix a restricted ad account, and it does not remove policy strikes. If a restriction exists, that is a separate appeal with its own evidence. The verification service exists to prepare the file once, correctly, rather than learning the rules through rejections.

If you have already been rejected

Do not resubmit the same file. Send the rejection notice, the documents used, and the exact business details in Business Manager for a review. Ask for an assessment — usually the mismatch is visible in a few minutes.
