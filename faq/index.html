FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Short answers about recovery, what to send, privacy and working together. If your question is not here, use the contact form.

General

Do you guarantee account recovery?

No. Recovery depends on the nature of the issue, platform policies, the evidence available, and decisions that Meta makes. Every case is evaluated individually, and I will tell you early what is realistic.

Recovery process

How long does recovery take?

It depends on the issue. Some cases resolve quickly. Others need more investigation or a longer platform review, and I cannot control how long Meta takes to respond.

What information do you need?

The service page lists what is needed for that service. In general, I ask for the account or Page name, when the problem started, any notices from Meta, and what you have already tried. I only ask for what is needed to assess and plan the case.

Do I need to share my password?

Not to start a conversation. Please do not send passwords, one-time codes, or recovery codes through the contact form or by message.

Privacy

How is the information I send handled?

The contact form sends your message through Web3Forms, a third-party form service, to my email. Share only what is needed for your case, and do not include passwords or codes.

Working with me

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. Remote services are available worldwide. I am based in Bangladesh (Asia/Dhaka, UTC+6), so please mention your time zone when we arrange a call.

Do you work with individuals or only businesses?

Both. I work with individual account holders, creators, page owners, businesses, and agencies. The service pages say who each service is for.

Still have a question?

Describe your situation in a few lines. I will tell you whether I can help and what the next step is.
