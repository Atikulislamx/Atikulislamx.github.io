Frequently Asked Questions

Honest answers about how recovery works, what to expect, and what I can and can't promise.

General

Do you guarantee account recovery?
No. Recovery depends on the nature of the issue, platform policies, available evidence, and many other factors. Every case is evaluated individually.

Recovery Process

How long does recovery take?
Recovery time depends entirely on the complexity of the issue. Some cases can be resolved quickly, while others may require additional investigation or platform review.
What information do you require?
The required information depends on the specific issue. Only information necessary for diagnosis and recovery will be requested.

Working With Me

Do you work with international clients?
Yes. Remote services are available for clients worldwide.
