Business Manager Verification: Anonymized Case

A digital marketing business faced repeated Business Manager restrictions and could not complete verification.

Meta Business ManagerDigital marketing businessMiddle East
Discuss a similar situationAll case studies

Problem

A digital marketing business faced repeated Business Manager restrictions and could not complete verification.

Situation

The business engaged me for Business Manager support.

Challenge

The Business Manager had several restrictions and verification issues.

Approach

I reviewed the restriction history and the current state of the Business Manager to find the path to verification.

Work

  • Reviewed the restriction history
  • Resolved the Business Manager restrictions
  • Prepared the Business Manager for Meta's verification process

Result

The Business Manager was verified.

Key takeaway

Verification is often blocked by unresolved restrictions rather than by the verification step itself. Resolving those first is usually the key step.

Service

  • Business Verification Guidance
  • Meta Business Manager Recovery

Limitations

  • Client details are withheld, so the case is described in general terms
  • Verification decisions are made by Meta. This case does not predict another outcome
