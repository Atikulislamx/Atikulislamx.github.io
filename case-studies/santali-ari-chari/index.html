Santali Ari-Chari: Case Study

Background

Santali Ari-Chari is a page dedicated to Santali language and culture.

Challenge

The page was compromised after being hacked.

Investigation

I assessed the compromise and the administrator's legitimate ownership of the page.

Solution

I completed account recovery and administrative restoration, then applied security improvements to reduce the risk of repeat compromise.

Outcome

The page was successfully recovered, restoring the community's online presence.

Key Takeaways

Recovering a compromised community page is about more than restoring access — securing it afterward is what protects the community it serves.
