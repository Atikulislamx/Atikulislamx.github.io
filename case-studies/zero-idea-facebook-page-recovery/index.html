Zero Idea: Facebook Page Recovery

A Facebook Page with a large educational audience was taken over, and the legitimate administrator lost access.

Facebook PageScience and educational content page
Discuss a similar situationAll case studies

Problem

A Facebook Page with a large educational audience was taken over, and the legitimate administrator lost access.

Situation

Zero Idea is a Facebook Page that publishes science and educational content, with over 400,000 followers.

Challenge

The page was hacked and the administrator was locked out for about two months.

Approach

I investigated how the compromise happened and confirmed the legitimate administrator's ownership of the page. Then I worked through the recovery route with the platform.

Work

  • Reviewed how the compromise occurred
  • Confirmed the legitimate administrator's ownership of the page
  • Worked through the recovery process to restore administrator access
  • Secured the page against further compromise once access was restored

Result

The page was recovered and the legitimate administrator regained control. The page owner later shared a public video acknowledging the recovery.

Key takeaway

Long lockouts can sometimes be resolved with a structured, patient process, even when an attacker has taken control. Results still vary from case to case.

Service

  • Facebook Page Recovery

Limitations

  • This case reflects one page and one set of circumstances. It does not predict the outcome of another case
  • Recovery depended on the evidence of ownership available and on platform decisions
