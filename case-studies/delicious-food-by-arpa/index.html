Delicious Food by Arpa: Case Study

Background

Delicious Food by Arpa is a food and cake business based in Bangladesh.

Challenge

The business faced fake copyright claims, a restricted advertising account, and Business Manager verification issues, all at once.

Investigation

I reviewed each issue individually — the copyright claims, the ad account restriction, and the verification blockers — to determine the correct resolution path for each.

Solution

I removed the false copyright issues, resolved the advertising restrictions, completed Business Manager verification, and assisted with Facebook Page verification.

Outcome

All requested objectives were successfully completed.

Key Takeaways

Multiple, compounding platform issues can often be resolved together as part of one structured engagement rather than requiring separate efforts.
