About Me

Atikul Islam Rabbi

Founder & CEO of Cyber Infinity — Social Media Security & Recovery Specialist

I am Atikul Islam Rabbi, the Founder & CEO of Cyber Infinity and a Social Media Security & Recovery Specialist. My work focuses on helping individuals, creators, businesses, and organizations recover compromised social media assets, strengthen account security, and resolve complex Meta platform issues.

I specialize in Facebook, Instagram, Meta Business Manager, and digital asset recovery. My approach combines technical knowledge, structured problem solving, and clear communication to deliver reliable and professional solutions.

Alongside my professional work, I continuously expand my knowledge in web development, cybersecurity, automation, artificial intelligence, and software engineering. I believe continuous learning is essential for staying ahead in the rapidly evolving digital landscape.

Mission

To help businesses, creators, and organizations recover, secure, and protect their digital presence through professional guidance, ethical practices, and security-first solutions.

Vision

To build a globally recognized digital security and consulting brand that empowers businesses with reliable recovery services, cybersecurity awareness, and innovative digital solutions.

Every problem deserves careful analysis before action. I believe in ethical practices, honest communication, continuous improvement, and delivering solutions that prioritize long-term security over short-term fixes.
