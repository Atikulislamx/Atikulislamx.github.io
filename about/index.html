About Atikul Islam Rabbi

Founder & CEO of Cyber Infinity and a Social Media Security & Recovery Specialist.

Describe your situationView services

I'm Atikul Islam Rabbi, Founder & CEO of Cyber Infinity. My work focuses on helping individuals, creators, businesses and organizations recover compromised social media assets, strengthen account security, and resolve complex Meta platform issues.

I specialize in Facebook, Instagram, Meta Business Manager and digital asset recovery. My approach combines technical knowledge, structured problem solving and clear communication, so you get reliable help with honest expectations.

Alongside this work, I keep learning in web development, cybersecurity, automation, artificial intelligence and software engineering. That learning supports the security work. It is not a separate service.
