About

Atikul Islam Rabbi

Social Media Security Expert and Founder & CEO of Cyber Infinity. I recover and protect the Meta assets that businesses cannot afford to lose.

Request an assessment Credentials & verification
Dhaka, Bangladesh (UTC+6) Remote worldwide 40+ critical assets recovered

I run a digital security practice built around one narrow, high-stakes problem: what happens when a business loses control of its social media assets. A Facebook Page that carries the brand. An Instagram account with the audience. A Meta Business Manager that holds the Pages, ad accounts, pixels and catalogs of an entire company.

Most of these incidents are not sophisticated attacks. They are an old administrator who was never removed, a profile without two-factor authentication, an agency handover that was never completed, or a shared password in a spreadsheet. The recovery is technical; the cause is usually operational.

That is why the work has two halves. The first is recovery: establishing what actually happened, assembling the evidence that proves legitimate ownership, and working the correct Meta route instead of repeating the same rejected appeal. The second is hardening: removing the access paths that made the takeover possible, so the same case does not return in six months.

I founded Cyber Infinity to deliver that work for founders, agencies and brands internationally. Clients range from a 400,000-follower education Page and an award-winning Edinburgh studio to investment firms and eCommerce brands operating across several markets.

I do not guarantee outcomes, because the final decision always sits with the platform. What I do guarantee is a structured review, a written assessment of the realistic routes, and no request for a password, a one-time code or a recovery code — ever.
