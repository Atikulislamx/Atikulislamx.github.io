The Problem

Many account compromises are preventable — weak admin structures, missing two-factor authentication, and unclear asset ownership all increase risk.

My Approach

I review the current security posture of your accounts and Business Manager, identify the specific gaps most likely to lead to compromise, and recommend practical steps to close them.

What You Can Expect

Consultation strengthens your security posture going forward; it does not retroactively address an active compromise, which falls under the relevant recovery service instead.

Before we start

Share the affected account or asset, when the issue began, and what steps you have already tried. If available, note any platform notices and what ownership records you can provide. We can discuss sensitive documentation through an appropriate channel after an initial review.

Never send your password, one-time login codes, or recovery codes through the contact form. Platform decisions, evidence requirements, and timelines vary; no result is guaranteed.
