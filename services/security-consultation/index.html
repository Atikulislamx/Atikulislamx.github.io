Security

Security Consultation

Advising businesses and creators on strengthening the security of their social media accounts and Business Manager setup.

Describe your situationAll services

The problem it solves

Many account compromises are preventable. Weak admin structures, missing two-factor authentication, and unclear asset ownership all increase risk.

Who it's for

  • Creators and businesses that want to reduce risk before an incident
  • Teams that have grown and are not sure who has access to what
  • Organizations that recently had an incident and want to harden the setup

My approach

I review the current security setup of your accounts and Business Manager, identify the gaps most likely to lead to a compromise, and recommend practical steps to close them.

How the process works

  1. 1

    Tell me your goals

    Describe the accounts in scope, who uses them, and what worries you most.

  2. 2

    I review the setup

    I review admin roles, two-factor settings, recovery contacts, and connected apps that you share with me.

  3. 3

    You get prioritized actions

    You receive the gaps ranked by risk, with the steps to close each one.

  4. 4

    We follow up if needed

    If you want help implementing the changes, we agree on that scope separately.

What to send

Only send what is needed for your case. Never send passwords, one-time codes or recovery codes.

  • The accounts and assets in scope
  • Who currently has access, and at what role
  • Any recent incidents or suspicious activity

Limitations

  • Consultation does not fix an active compromise. That falls under the relevant recovery service
  • Recommendations are only as complete as the information you share
  • Platform settings change over time, so advice reflects the date of review
