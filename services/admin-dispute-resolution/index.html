Admin Dispute Resolution

Resolving disputes over Page or Business Manager administrator access and asset ownership.

Describe your situationAll services

The problem it solves

Disputes over who legitimately administers a Page or Business Manager can leave assets frozen or contested, especially after a change in team, a former partner keeping access, or a takeover attempt.

Who it's for

  • Businesses where a former partner or employee still controls assets
  • Teams with conflicting admin claims after a takeover
  • Owners who need to re-establish control of a Page or Business Manager

My approach

I review the ownership and administrative history of the asset, help establish legitimate ownership through Meta's dispute and verification channels, and work toward a resolution that restores proper control.

How the process works

  1. 1

    Tell me who is involved

    Describe the asset, the people involved, and the history you know about.

  2. 2

    I review the history

    I review the admin and ownership record to find what can be shown and by whom.

  3. 3

    You get a written plan

    You receive the evidence to gather, the Meta route to use, and the likely next steps.

  4. 4

    We submit and follow the case

    We submit through Meta's channels and track each response until a decision is made.

What to send

Only send what is needed for your case. Never send passwords, one-time codes or recovery codes.

  • The asset name and ID
  • The admin history you know about, and who was involved
  • Documents that show ownership or authority
  • Any communication with the other party or with Meta about the dispute

Limitations

  • Disputes are decided by Meta under its own evidence rules
  • Outcomes depend on the evidence available and on the other party's position
  • I cannot compel another person to hand over access

Related questions

Do you guarantee account recovery?

No. Recovery depends on the nature of the issue, platform policies, the evidence available, and decisions that Meta makes. Every case is evaluated individually, and I will tell you early what is realistic.

What information do you need?

The service page lists what is needed for that service. In general, I ask for the account or Page name, when the problem started, any notices from Meta, and what you have already tried. I only ask for what is needed to assess and plan the case.
