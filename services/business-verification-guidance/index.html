Business Verification Guidance

Guiding businesses through Meta Business Manager verification, including resolving restrictions that block the process.

Request this service All services

The problem it solves

Meta's business verification can be blocked by earlier restrictions, unclear requirements, or incomplete documents, which leaves legitimate businesses stuck.

Who it is for

  • Businesses stuck at the verification step
  • Agencies verifying a client's Business Manager
  • Businesses whose verification was rejected and who need to prepare again

My approach

I review the current state of the Business Manager, resolve any restrictions standing in the way, and guide the business through preparing for and completing Meta's verification.

How the process works

  1. 1

    Tell me where you are stuck

    Share the verification message you see and the documents you plan to submit.

  2. 2

    I review the Business Manager

    I check for restrictions and inconsistencies that could block verification.

  3. 3

    You get a written plan

    You receive the restrictions to clear, the documents needed, and the order to submit them.

  4. 4

    We submit and follow up

    We submit once the setup is ready, then track Meta's response and handle any follow-up request.

What to send

Only send what is needed for your case. Never send passwords, one-time codes or recovery codes.

  • The legal business name and registration details
  • The business website and domain, if any
  • The business documents you plan to submit
  • Any earlier verification rejection messages

Limitations

  • I cannot create or alter business documents
  • Verification decisions are made by Meta, not by me
  • Some restrictions take time to clear
No guarantees. The final decision sits with the platform. What I control is the quality of the evidence and the correctness of the route.
