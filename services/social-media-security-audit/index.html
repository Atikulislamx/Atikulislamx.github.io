Security

Social Media Security Audit

A structured review of your social media and Business Manager accounts to identify security risks before they become incidents.

Contact Me About This

The Problem

Businesses often don't have visibility into who has access to their Pages, ad accounts, and Business Manager, or which settings leave them exposed.

My Approach

I audit administrator access, asset ownership, and account-level security settings across your Facebook, Instagram, and Business Manager presence, and provide a clear summary of findings and recommendations.

What You Can Expect

An audit identifies risk areas and recommended fixes; it is a preventative service, distinct from active account recovery.
