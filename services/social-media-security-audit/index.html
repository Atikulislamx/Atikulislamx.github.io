Security

Social Media Security Audit

A structured review of your social media and Business Manager accounts to identify security risks before they become incidents.

Describe your situationAll services

The problem it solves

Businesses often lack a clear view of who has access to their Pages, ad accounts, and Business Manager, or of which settings leave them exposed.

Who it's for

  • Businesses with several admins, Pages, and ad accounts
  • Agencies that manage assets for clients
  • Teams preparing for growth, a handover, or a change of staff

My approach

I review administrator access, asset ownership, and account-level security settings across your Facebook, Instagram, and Business Manager presence, then provide a clear summary of findings and recommendations.

How the process works

  1. 1

    Define the scope

    We agree which Pages, ad accounts, Business Managers, and Instagram accounts are in scope.

  2. 2

    Review access and settings

    I review admin and editor roles, asset ownership, and security settings for each asset.

  3. 3

    You get a prioritized report

    You receive the findings ranked by risk, with the fix for each one.

  4. 4

    Optional follow-up

    If you want help making the changes, we agree on that scope separately.

What to send

Only send what is needed for your case. Never send passwords, one-time codes or recovery codes.

  • A list of Pages, ad accounts, Business Managers, and Instagram accounts
  • The current admin and editor list, by asset
  • Any known issues or concerns

Limitations

  • An audit identifies risks and fixes. It does not recover accounts
  • Findings reflect what was reviewed on the date of the audit
  • Some checks depend on the access level you can share
