Instagram Account Recovery

Recovering compromised or inaccessible Instagram accounts.

Describe your situationAll services

The problem it solves

Instagram account takeovers can lock creators and businesses out of their audience, their content history, and any Meta Business assets linked to the account.

Who it's for

  • Creators and brands whose audience and content history are at risk
  • Businesses whose Instagram account is linked to a Meta Business Manager
  • Account owners locked out after a takeover or a change of email or phone number

My approach

I investigate the compromise, guide the account-ownership verification process with Meta, and work toward restoring legitimate access, then reduce the chance of a repeat.

How the process works

  1. 1

    Tell me what happened

    Share the username, the contact details on the account, and what you noticed first.

  2. 2

    I assess the account

    I check how access was lost and whether the account is linked to a Page or Business Manager.

  3. 3

    You get a written plan

    You receive the verification steps, what each needs from you, and which steps only Meta can complete.

  4. 4

    We restore access and secure it

    We work through the verification steps, then review login alerts, recovery contacts, and two-factor settings.

What to send

Only send what is needed for your case. Never send passwords, one-time codes or recovery codes.

  • The Instagram username
  • The email address or phone number linked to the account
  • Any login alerts or emails you received
  • Whether the account is linked to a Facebook Page or Business Manager

Limitations

  • Recovery depends on Meta's ownership verification, which is outside my control
  • Accounts with earlier policy violations may face additional review
  • I do not work around platform controls

What you can expect

Related questions

Do you guarantee account recovery?

No. Recovery depends on the nature of the issue, platform policies, the evidence available, and decisions that Meta makes. Every case is evaluated individually, and I will tell you early what is realistic.

How long does recovery take?

It depends on the issue. Some cases resolve quickly. Others need more investigation or a longer platform review, and I cannot control how long Meta takes to respond.

What information do you need?

The service page lists what is needed for that service. In general, I ask for the account or Page name, when the problem started, any notices from Meta, and what you have already tried. I only ask for what is needed to assess and plan the case.

  3. 3
