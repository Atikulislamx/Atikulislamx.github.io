Instagram Account Recovery

Recovering compromised or inaccessible Instagram accounts.

Contact Me About This

The Problem

Instagram account takeovers can lock out creators and businesses from their audience, content history, and any linked Meta Business assets.

My Approach

I investigate the compromise, guide the account-ownership verification process with Meta, and work toward restoring legitimate access.

What You Can Expect

Every case is evaluated individually, and I do not guarantee recovery — but I apply a consistent, evidence-based process to each one.

Related Questions

Do you guarantee account recovery?
No. Recovery depends on the nature of the issue, platform policies, available evidence, and many other factors. Every case is evaluated individually.
How long does recovery take?
Recovery time depends entirely on the complexity of the issue. Some cases can be resolved quickly, while others may require additional investigation or platform review.
What information do you require?
The required information depends on the specific issue. Only information necessary for diagnosis and recovery will be requested.
