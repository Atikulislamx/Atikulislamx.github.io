Facebook Page Recovery

Recovering ownership and administrator access to Facebook Pages that have been compromised, hijacked, or lost.

Request this service All services

The problem it solves

Facebook Pages are frequently targeted for takeover, which can lock the legitimate administrator out while the page's audience and reputation remain at risk.

Who it is for

  • Page owners whose administrator access was removed or changed
  • Businesses and creators who can no longer manage a Page they own
  • Organizations whose Page was taken over by a former team member or an attacker

My approach

I investigate how the compromise happened, work through Meta's review and verification processes, and focus on restoring legitimate administrator access and securing the page once control is regained.

How the process works

  1. 1

    Tell me what happened

    Use the contact form to describe the problem, when it started, and what you have already tried.

  2. 2

    I review the evidence

    I check the Page's admin history, the proof of ownership you have, and any Meta notices you received.

  3. 3

    You get a written plan

    You receive a summary of the routes open for your case, what each one needs, and what I recommend.

  4. 4

    We work the case and secure it

    We work through Meta's review steps, then tighten admin roles and two-factor settings once access is restored.

What to send

Only send what is needed for your case. Never send passwords, one-time codes or recovery codes.

  • The Page name and URL
  • The personal account that is, or was, an admin, if you know it
  • When you first noticed the problem, and any Meta notices or emails you received
  • Any proof of ownership you have, such as business documents, past admin invitations, or screenshots

Limitations

  • Recovery depends on Meta's review, which I cannot control or speed up
  • A Page with unresolved policy restrictions can be harder to recover
  • I can only work to restore access that you can show is legitimately yours
No guarantees. The final decision sits with the platform. What I control is the quality of the evidence and the correctness of the route.
