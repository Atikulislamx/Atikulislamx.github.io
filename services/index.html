Process

How an engagement runs

The same four steps for every service, so you always know what happens next and what it needs from you.

  1. 1

    Tell me what happened

    Use the contact form. Add the timeline, the platform and any notices you received. No passwords.

  2. 2

    I review the evidence

    Ownership proof, admin history and the route that is actually open for your case.

  1. 3

    You get a written plan

    The options, what each one needs from you, and my recommendation. You decide.

  2. 4

    We work the case and secure it

    Recovery first, then access hardening so the same failure cannot repeat.

What I will not do: ask for a password or one-time code, promise a guaranteed recovery, or take on an account you cannot show is yours.
