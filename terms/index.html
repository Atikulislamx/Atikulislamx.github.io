Advisory and technical work relating to social media account security, recovery, verification and disputes — principally for Facebook, Instagram and Meta Business Manager. The scope of each engagement is agreed in writing before work begins.

3. Your responsibilities

  • You must be the legitimate owner, or authorised to act for the owner, of every asset you ask me to work on.
